Incident Response and Notificationvendor_security_standard.md

Suppliers should maintain a documented incident response process, named escalation contacts, and a contractual notification requirement. Security incidents affecting organizational data, systems, or service continuity should be communicated without unnecessary delay.

Supplier Access and Authenticationvendor_security_standard.md

Third-party access to organizational systems should be approved, limited to a documented business need, and periodically reviewed. Multi-factor authentication should be required for remote and privileged supplier access. Supplier accounts should follow least-privilege principles and be disabled when access is no longer required.

Due Diligence and Monitoringc_scrm_framework_notes.md

Supplier due diligence should examine the context of the relationship, access, data, service criticality, evidence, and downstream dependencies. Higher-risk suppliers warrant stronger assessment, contractual requirements, monitoring, and reassessment.
