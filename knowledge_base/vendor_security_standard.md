# Supplier Access and Authentication
Third-party access to organizational systems should be approved, limited to a documented business need, and periodically reviewed. Multi-factor authentication should be required for remote and privileged supplier access. Supplier accounts should follow least-privilege principles and be disabled when access is no longer required.

# Vulnerability and Patch Management
Suppliers with connected systems should maintain a documented vulnerability-remediation process. Critical or actively exploited vulnerabilities should receive accelerated remediation. Patch evidence should be reviewable during due diligence or reassessment.

# Encryption and Data Protection
Sensitive supplier-accessed organizational data should be encrypted in transit and at rest where applicable. Data access should be limited to the minimum information required for the contracted service.

# Backup and Recovery
Critical suppliers should maintain backup and recovery procedures appropriate to the supported service. Recovery capability should be tested rather than assumed. Restore exercises should produce evidence that can be reviewed during risk assessment.

# Incident Response and Notification
Suppliers should maintain a documented incident response process, named escalation contacts, and a contractual notification requirement. Security incidents affecting organizational data, systems, or service continuity should be communicated without unnecessary delay.
