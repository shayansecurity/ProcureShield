# Evidence Status
A missing answer is not automatically proof of compliance or noncompliance. When evidence is absent or stale, the assessment should identify the item as Not Verified and treat the uncertainty consistently according to the documented scoring model.

# Risk Tiers and Escalation
Low-risk findings may be accepted or monitored through normal review. Medium-risk findings should have a documented owner and remediation plan where appropriate. High-risk findings should receive prioritized remediation and management visibility. Critical-risk findings should trigger immediate escalation, documented treatment decisions, and reassessment after remediation.

# Assessment Traceability
A supplier assessment should record the input data, model version, calculated score, key risk drivers, supporting evidence, generated narrative, and assessment timestamp. This supports repeatability, auditability, and later comparison.
